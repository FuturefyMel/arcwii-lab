How this lab works

  1. Type your full name in Student name first — your work is saved under your name.
  2. Your answers and uploaded images then autosave to this browser only as you type.

Sharing a computer with classmates? Each student's work is kept separate by the name typed in Student name — type your own full name every time so you don't see (or overwrite) someone else's answers. Nothing here is stored on a server, so don't clear your browser data before you've downloaded your PDF.
