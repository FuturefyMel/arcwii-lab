How this lab works

  1. Enter your name and campaign/project once on any tool page — it carries over to the others.
  2. Your answers and uploaded images autosave to this browser only as you type.
  3. When an assignment is ready, click Download PDF and upload that file to Google Classroom. That's your submission.
  4. Nothing here is stored on a server, so stick to the same browser and computer for a given assignment, and don't clear your browser data before you've downloaded your PDF.
