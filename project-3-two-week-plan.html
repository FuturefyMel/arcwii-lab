Project 3: Content Calendar and Campaign Management Plan

A detailed two-week window of 8–10 posts, plus your team's workflow, timeline, quality checklist, and community response plan.

Scenario: SMART Shoe

You are part of the social media marketing team for SMART Shoe. Your team has been assigned to plan and manage a two-week social media campaign promoting SMART Shoe. All campaign content should be static image posts.

Assume you are working with the following five-person team. Use these five team members when assigning responsibility for each post — consider each person's role when deciding who should be responsible.

Team memberRole
MayaSocial Media Manager — oversees the campaign, manages the content calendar, schedules posts, and gives final approval.
JordanContent Strategist — develops content ideas, selects content pillars, and ensures content supports campaign goals.
TaylorGraphic Designer — creates the static graphics for social media.
ChrisCopywriter — writes captions and calls to action.
AlexisCommunity Manager — monitors comments and messages and responds to the online community.

Two-week posts

Part 2: Team Approval Workflow

Explain your team's approval workflow. Show how a social media post moves from the initial idea to publication. Identify which team member is responsible at each stage.

For example: Jordan develops the content idea → Chris writes the caption → Taylor creates the graphic → Maya reviews the completed post → Revisions are made if needed → Maya gives final approval → Maya schedules and publishes the post → Alexis monitors comments and engagement.

Part 3: Campaign Timeline

Identify when campaign creation will begin and when it will finish. Enter the date your team will complete each step.

Part 4: Quality Checklist

Create a checklist your team will use before approving a post for publication. Include at least 5 items. Consider branding, spelling and grammar, image quality, caption accuracy, calls to action, and whether the content supports the campaign goal.

Part 5: Community Response Plan

Create a brief plan explaining how Alexis, the Community Manager, should manage the SMART Shoe online community during the campaign. Explain how the brand should handle positive comments, product questions, negative comments, customer complaints, and spam or inappropriate comments. Also determine how quickly your team should respond and when Alexis should escalate an issue to Maya, the Social Media Manager.
